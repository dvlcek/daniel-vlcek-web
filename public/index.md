# Daniel VLKO

Official website: https://danielvlko.com/

## Overview

Daniel VLKO is a Software Developer & Automation Architect focused on building scalable digital systems for businesses.

The work combines custom software development, automation, AI-assisted workflows and digital platforms to help companies operate faster, reduce repetitive manual work and build more scalable processes.

## Positioning

Daniel VLKO operates as a technical growth partner and systems builder.

The focus is not isolated development tasks. The objective is to understand the business process, identify bottlenecks and build digital infrastructure that creates long-term operational leverage.

### Custom Software Development

Design and development of custom digital systems built around real business requirements, workflows and operational needs.

### Business Process Automation

Automation of repetitive processes, manual handoffs, data movement, internal workflows and operational tasks.

### AI-Assisted Workflows

Practical integration of artificial intelligence into business processes where AI can improve speed, efficiency, decision support or operational capacity.

Development of scalable digital platforms designed around business operations, growth and long-term maintainability.

High-performance websites, digital experiences and custom web solutions built with a focus on speed, usability, scalability and business outcomes.

## Approach

Projects are approached as systems rather than isolated tasks.

Typical work begins by understanding the current workflow, identifying where time, information or efficiency is being lost and determining where software, automation or AI can create meaningful leverage.

Solutions are designed with scalability, maintainability and long-term business value in mind.
